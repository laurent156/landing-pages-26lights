# Image brief — "Why Odoo" illustration (26lights)

## Context for the image
Replaces a real Odoo dashboard screenshot on a B2B tech-consultancy landing page. The section's headline is **"Infinitely modular, just like your business."** — the image should read as *modularity / apps connecting into one system*, ideally still feeling like it belongs next to real product screenshots (the other two sections on the same page keep real Odoo screenshots), not like an abstract technical diagram.

**What didn't work, avoid repeating:** a dark glowing hub-and-spoke line diagram (magenta center node, six outlined squares connected by thin lines, radial glow). It read as generic/tech-diagram, not specific to Odoo or to a real product, and didn't sit well next to the real screenshots elsewhere on the page.

## Brand system
- **Accent color (this page only):** magenta `#DB187C` (bright variant `#FE3B9B` for glows/highlights on dark). This is the *only* accent color — never introduce a second hue.
- **Neutrals:** ink `#0A0A0A`, near-black background `#050505`, light gray `#F5F5F5`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- **Typeface (for any on-brand UI chrome shown inside the image, not for real text):** Kumbh Sans — a clean, rounded-but-not-playful geometric sans.
- **Overall brand mood:** confident, clean, senior/professional B2B SaaS — not playful, not cartoonish, not cyberpunk/neon.

## Direction to ask for
- **Style:** the clean, soft, modern illustration style used by SaaS marketing sites like Stripe, Linear, or Notion — soft 3D/isometric blocks or a stylized (but plausible-looking) app UI, not a flat technical line diagram and not literal clip-art icons.
- **Background:** light — white or very pale neutral (`#F5F5F5`-ish). Not a dark/glowing card. The section it sits in is a light page.
- **Color use:** the magenta accent (`#DB187C`) as the one highlight color (a few blocks, a button, an accent shape) against neutral grays/whites — not the dominant fill of the whole image.
- **Composition idea:** several distinct "module" blocks or app tiles (each subtly different, like real app icons/panels) visually assembling, stacking, or plugging into one unified surface/dashboard — communicating "many parts, one system" concretely rather than abstractly.
- **Format:** landscape, roughly 4:3 or 16:10, no baked-in text or logos, generous empty margin so it can be cropped/inset into a card frame.

## What to avoid
- Neon/dark backgrounds, glow effects, sci-fi/circuit-board motifs.
- Generic gradient blobs or stock "network/connection" clip-art.
- More than one accent hue.
- Any readable text, wordmarks, or UI copy in the image.
- Photorealistic renders of unrelated software (must not look like Notion/Salesforce/etc. specifically — keep it generic-but-plausible SaaS UI).
